> - Cultural landmark serving traditional café fare
> - Try the medialunas (Argentine croissants) and hot chocolate
> - Great for breakfast or afternoon coffee
>
> **3. [La Cocina Porteña](/restaurants?id=rest-arg-007)** - $35/person ⭐ 4.5

> - Fresh empanadas made daily and traditional specials
> - Excellent milanesas and locro
> - True porteño experience at budget prices
>
> All three offer exceptional food for the price. El Cuartito is perfect for a quick, filling meal, while La Cocina Porteña gives you a more traditional Argentine dining experience. Would you like recommendations for specific meal types?

## Key Features Demonstrated

### ✅ Real Data
- All prices, IDs, and details come from actual data files
- Accommodations filter for budget/mid-range only
- Flights show only economy and premium-economy

### ✅ Clickable Links
- Every recommendation includes a properly formatted link
- Links use the pattern: `[Name](/category?id=ITEM_ID)`
- Links keep users within the concierge chat

### ✅ Budget Focus
- Agent emphasizes value and affordability
- Calculates total costs to keep users under budget
- Suggests money-saving alternatives

### ✅ Helpful Context
- Explains why options are good value
- Provides ratings and amenities
- Offers alternatives at different price points

### ✅ Conversational Tone
- Warm and friendly personality
- Asks follow-up questions
- Provides complete trip planning assistance
